# ElectroSpot: marketplace direction and Phase 1

## Aim

- Homeowners: estimate electrical equipment costs, compare brands and order directly.
- Electricians and contractors: buy supplies for their customers, earn purchase rewards and return for the next project.
- Enable remote purchasing; confirm serviceable locations rather than promising universal delivery.

## Current pilot versus intended product

The pilot offers brand-specific browsing and a mixed-brand quotation basket. Buyers identify themselves as homeowners, electricians or contractors and can add a project reference. Catalog prices are on request. The existing home-size estimator uses illustrative prices, not supplier quotations.

Direct checkout, payments, order tracking and instant rewards are future work. The proposed 20% discount needs supplier margins, a clear reference price, eligibility, redemption rules and cancellation/refund treatment before launch. Do not present the proposal as an active entitlement.

## Phase 1: validate over 2–3 weeks

1. Week 1: interview homeowners with upcoming construction and electricians/contractors with active projects. Record participant segment, current purchasing method, project timing and biggest difficulty before showing the concept. Suggested initial sample: 20 people in each audience; keep results separate.
2. Week 2: show the pilot and ask each person to assemble a real project quotation. Record completion, missing products, preferred brands, expected delivery area and willingness to request a supplier quote.
3. Week 3, if needed: revisit unclear responses, obtain supplier pricing and test whether the proposed offer is financially sustainable. Seek explicit consent before follow-up; keep contact details separate from anonymized interview notes.

## Questions

Electricians/contractors: “Would you use an app that gave you 20% discounts + instant rewards?” Explain that this is a proposed offer, not available today. Follow with: “Compared with which current price?”, “What does instant mean to you?”, “Would you use it without that discount?”, and “Which upcoming purchase would you try first?”

Homeowners: “Would you use an app to estimate electrical equipment costs?” Follow with: “How do you estimate today?”, “Which brands would you compare?”, and “Would you share a real materials list for a quotation?”

## Decision record

Use the founder's target of at least 80% YES in each audience as an initial interest gate: YES / all people asked in that audience. Record NO and UNSURE separately; count UNSURE in the denominator. For 20 participants, at least 16 YES meets this gate. This is a pilot target, not proof of demand.

Also record actual quotation attempts, completed requests, willingness to buy at confirmed prices and supplier margins. A positive interview answer alone should not trigger investment in full checkout or a guaranteed reward scheme.
